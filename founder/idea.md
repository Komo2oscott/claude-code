# DIRTY is Better ("Dirty Greens")

- What it is: field-packed, unwashed leafy greens sold in grocery stores with a mesh wash bag in the container, so the customer washes them at home instead of buying chlorine-washed bagged salad.
- Who it is for: grocery shoppers who buy bagged salad greens and want fresher-tasting, less processed produce (the brand's tone, merch and TikTok/Instagram presence point to younger, style-conscious shoppers). Not yet specified by the founder.
- What it sells, at what price: four greens, Sir Leafs-A-Lot Arugula, Rosie's Iron Will Spinach, Kali-fornia Baby Kale, Astro Greens Mixed Leaf.
